# VerdeAI v10.2.0 — First Real Tester Release

## Goal
Find out whether ordinary people understand VerdeAI, believe the six same-property futures, and want to try it with their own property.

## First-tester journey
1. Open the finished shaded side-access example.
2. Explore the whole board or compare the six futures.
3. Answer three quick questions.
4. Save the answers locally.
5. Tap **Copy answers to send back** and paste them into a message to the owner.

## The three questions
- Did the VerdeAI idea make sense?
- Did the six futures feel believable?
- Would you try VerdeAI with your own property?

No name, account, login, or written explanation is requested.

## New-photo honesty
The secondary landing action is **Try your own photo — early preview**. The page states that automatic future-image creation for new photos is not connected. The browser-only planning and manual same-property workflow remain available.

## Owner evidence
The existing local feedback review area includes **Download first-tester CSV**. It exports the focused three-question records stored in that browser.

## Safety
Real AI rendering is disabled. Backend disconnected. Provider calls off. Paid calls locked. Kill switch on. No frontend API key. Photos and tester responses remain in the browser unless deliberately copied or exported.
