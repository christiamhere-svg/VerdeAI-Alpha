# VerdeAI v9.8.0 — Golden Same-Property Demo Board

## Acceptance target
The completed example must feel much closer to the original aspirational VerdeAI board:
- the same starting property remains recognisable;
- six futures visibly transform that same place;
- each future has a distinct emotional and practical identity;
- the five-year strip continues the same property story;
- unrelated stock-property images do not carry the main demo experience.

## Demo future mapping
- Feature Garden → strong focal composition and water feature
- Low-Maintenance Haven → calm layered arrival with restrained lighting
- Wildlife Haven → lush habitat and pollinator planting
- Gathering Space → seating, warmth, and evening lighting
- Food Garden → productive edible garden beds
- Maker / Workshop Yard → practical studio/workshop destination

## Important honesty boundary
These are bundled, pre-generated demonstration assets. They show the intended VerdeAI experience to a first tester without activating live rendering.

A new user’s uploaded photograph is not yet transformed into six photorealistic images. That later capability requires a server-controlled image-rendering connection and remains locked.
